Mission Statement

The Problem

Problem 1:
Many students are often required to juggle multiple websites and applications in an online school (e.g. an LMS, a chatting app, a video conferencing app, and an email, ), at the same time many students are young or facing disabilities, and are not the most familiar with technology. This not only makes it difficult for students to learn how to use the website/app as they are now required to learn multiple platforms, but they may also have trouble keeping up to date with the latest announcements.

Problem 2:
Educational institutions are finding it difficult to provide interactive and coherent online learning. Conventional online systems lack interactive features, which lead to poor student engagement and module completion. Furthermore, monitoring the progress of students and on-time module completion is also an issue.

Why is this significant?
Students struggle to stay motivated in self-paced learning environments.
The instructors are not able to monitor and direct the students suitably.
Institutions need a scalable solution to improve student performance and retention

Our Solution

Our solution is to create a single platform that combines all the features of the various platforms into one, making it easier for students to access and use. This platform will be designed with accessibility in mind, ensuring that it is easy to use for all students, regardless of their abilities. Additionally, the platform will include interactive features that will help to engage students and improve their learning experience. The platform will also include tools for instructors to monitor student progress and provide feedback, ensuring that students are on track to complete their modules on time.
